# GST Billing App - Feature Roadmap & TODO

> Last Updated: 2026-01-25

## 🎯 High Priority (Quick Wins for Sales)

- [ ] **Multi-business Support** - Allow users to manage multiple businesses/brands from one app with separate GSTIN, logo, and settings
- [ ] **GST Returns Reports** - Auto-generate GSTR-1/3B summaries (JSON export format)
- [ ] **E-Invoice (IRN) Integration** - Connect to GST portal for IRN generation (mandatory for ₹5Cr+ businesses)
- [ ] **E-Way Bill Generation** - Required for goods transport above ₹50k
- [ ] **WhatsApp/Email Invoice Sharing** - One-tap share directly to customer's WhatsApp

---

## 🔄 Medium Priority (Competitive Differentiation)

- [ ] **Payment Tracking** - Mark invoices as Paid/Unpaid/Partial, payment reminders
- [ ] **Customer Ledger** - Outstanding balance per customer, aging analysis
- [ ] **Purchase Invoices** - Track purchases for complete input/output GST calculation
- [ ] **Quotation/Estimate Generation** - Create quotes that convert to invoices with one tap
- [ ] **Credit Notes/Debit Notes** - Handle returns and adjustments (GST compliance)
- [ ] **Recurring Invoices** - Auto-generate invoices for subscription/regular customers
- [ ] **Barcode/QR Scanner** - Scan products for quick addition to invoice
- [ ] **Multi-user Access** - Staff login with role-based permissions (view/edit/admin)
- [ ] **Expense Tracking** - Track business expenses for profit/loss overview

---

## 🎨 UI/UX Improvements

- [ ] **Dashboard/Analytics Screen** - Sales trends, top products, GST summary charts
- [ ] **Thermal Printer Support** - 58mm/80mm receipt printing for POS use case
- [ ] **Voice/Camera Input** - Add products by speaking or scanning
- [ ] **Quick Invoice Mode** - Minimal-tap invoice for repeat/counter sales
- [ ] **Customizable Invoice Templates** - Multiple PDF designs to choose from
- [ ] **Offline-first with Smart Sync** - Works fully offline, syncs when connected

---

## 💰 Business/Monetization Features

- [ ] **Free + Premium Tiers** - Free: Limited invoices/month, Premium: Unlimited
- [ ] **White-labeling** - Let businesses use their own branding in app
- [ ] **Desktop Companion App** - Web/desktop version synced with mobile
- [ ] **Reports Export** - Export to Tally, Excel, PDF reports
- [ ] **SMS/Email Reminders** - Payment reminder automation (premium feature)

---

## ⭐ Recommended Starting Point

Start with these 5 features for maximum impact with minimum effort:

1. [ ] **GST Returns Helper (GSTR-1/3B JSON export)** - Huge pain point, direct value
2. [ ] **Payment Tracking with Ledger** - Makes the app sticky, daily use
3. [ ] **WhatsApp Integration** - Sharing via WhatsApp is essential in India
4. [ ] **Dashboard with Analytics** - Professional look, shows business insights
5. [ ] **Quotation → Invoice Flow** - Complete sales cycle management

---

## 📝 Notes

- Priority based on market demand and development effort
- E-Invoice and E-Way Bill are government mandates - high value for B2B customers
- WhatsApp sharing is culturally critical for Indian market
- Payment tracking converts the app from "invoice generator" to "business management tool"
